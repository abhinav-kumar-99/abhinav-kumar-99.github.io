I am a Ph.D. student in Robotics at the University of Michigan at Ann Arbor, working in Professor Dmitry Berenson's [ARM Lab](https://arm.eecs.umich.edu/).

My research focuses on precise, dexterous manipulation with multi-fingered hands. I have worked heavily on applying diffusion/flow matching techniques to multi-fingered hands, combining them with other ideas like trajectory optimization, graph-based search, and world models. A throughline is bringing enough structure relating to contact mechanics to make the learning problem easier and allow us to outperform data we train on, while still leveraging the strength and flexibility of learning methods. In the past, I have also done work on ensuring constraint satisfaction of machine learning models to make them more useful in robotics where hard constraint satisfaction and safety are important.

In my free time, I enjoy climbing, pretending I'm a film buff, and reading: mostly sci-fi, fantasy, and history. I enjoy writing as well, which you can find on my Substack, [I Like to Think](https://liketothink.substack.com/). Relatedly, I also enjoy table-top role playing games like Dungeons and Dragons as well as [thinking about the math](https://medium.com/new-writers-welcome/exploring-dice-math-with-explosions-6d558e36bfe5) behind them.
